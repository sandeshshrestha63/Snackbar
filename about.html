Our story

Eat, drink,
and be merry.

A small coffee and snacks bar built around one phrase, and a second outlet already open.

☕

We kept it simple on purpose: coffee, food, corn dogs, and a short drinks list. No long story about family kitchens or secret recipes here - just a place that wanted to do a few things well.

Corn dogs made the menu because they're the kind of snack that makes people smile when a tray lands on the table. Coffee made it because mornings need it. And a Virgin Mojito made it because not every drink needs alcohol to be worth ordering.

We opened a second outlet because people kept turning up at the first one. That is really the whole story so far - the rest we are still writing, one cup and one corn dog at a time.

🍹
What we care about

Three things we
keep coming back to

  1. Keep it simple

    Coffee, corndogs, snacks, drinks. We would rather do a short menu well than a long one badly.

  2. Make people comfortable

    "Eat, drink, and be merry" is not just a line in our bio. It is what we are going for every time you sit down.

  3. Stay easy to reach

    Call us, message us on Instagram, or just walk in. We will always make time for you.

Eat, drink, and be merry isn't a slogan we picked for the wall - it's just how we'd like you to leave.
